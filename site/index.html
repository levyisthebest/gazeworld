01 / OVERVIEW

Beyond where.
Toward what comes next.

Expert eye movements capture a process: searching, comparing, and gathering evidence. GazeWorld turns this process into a learning signal by predicting the representation of the next fixated image patch, while learning to complete the regions left unexplored.

Pretraining uses radiologist gaze. Downstream inference needs only the image. The learned representations support diagnostic classification and scanpath prediction with frozen visual features.
